# Greysound Pro 1.0.1

Keyboard and plug-in fixes for 1.0.

### Keyboard

- After you switch to another app and back, Space and the other shortcuts work right away, without clicking in the window first.
- With a plug-in window in front, Space and the edit shortcuts act in the session, as in Pro Tools. Keys go into the plug-in only while you are typing in one of its value fields.

### Plug-ins

- If the plug-in list fails to load when a session opens, Greysound Pro tries again on its own. The insert menu no longer stays at "No plugins available" until you rescan.

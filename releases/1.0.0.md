# Greysound Pro 1.0

Greysound Pro is out of beta. 1.0 brings MIDI editing and automation that work the way they do in Pro Tools, Pro Tools keyboard shortcuts, clip mute, and a faster, steadier desktop app.

### MIDI editing

- A full MIDI Editor in its own window, with several tracks at once and the Grabber, Selector, Trim, Pencil (with note shapes) and Smart tools. Keep it on top of the main window or not, in Settings > Windows.
- Edit notes, velocities and controllers right on the timeline: choose Notes, Velocity or a controller view from a track's Track View.
- Event List, Event Operations (quantize, groove templates, transpose and more), Real-Time Properties, Step Input, Chord and Key Change, and a Score Editor with notation. Each opens as a window of its own.
- MIDI recording works like Pro Tools: Merge or Replace, loop recording with Merge (each pass plays back on the next), count-in, MIDI Thru and input monitoring. A locate, stop or mute ends the notes and sustain that are sounding, never a key you are still holding.
- Controllers are chased across the whole track, and MIDI import and export keep trims, loops and tempo.

### Automation

- Automation lanes under each track, with Write, Touch, Latch and Trim modes, Preview, Capture, and Write, Glide and Trim to Current.
- The Automation window (View > Automation Window, Command+keypad 4) opens as its own window.
- Automate plug-in parameters; plug-in controls show their automation state in colour, as in Pro Tools.
- Edit lanes with every tool, draw shapes with the Pencil, thin breakpoints, and let automation follow your clip edits.

### Pro Tools shortcuts

- The Pro Tools shortcut scheme now uses Pro Tools' own keys: keypad transport, the Edit tools on Command+1–7, zoom, nudge, track height, the menu keys and Commands Keyboard Focus.

### Clips

- Command+M mutes and unmutes the selected clips. A muted clip is dimmed, plays nothing, and is left out of MIDI export.

### Faster and steadier

- AI features, previews, stem splits, AutoVox and dropped files keep audio on disk instead of loading it into the app, so long sessions use much less memory. Large uploads stream from disk.
- Plug-in windows float above the main window and stack correctly with dialogs and menus.
- A track set to No Output plays its sends but stays silent at the Master, live and in bounces, as in Pro Tools.

### Look and feel

- The app uses its own pickers and checkboxes throughout, menus stay inside the window and show when they scroll, and top-bar controls that do not fit move into a » shelf.
- The CoProducer panel starts collapsed in narrow windows.

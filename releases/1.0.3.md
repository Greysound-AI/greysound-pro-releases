# Greysound Pro 1.0.3

New plans, simpler pricing, and subscribing from Pro works again.

### New plans

- Four plans: Plus (2,400 credits a week), Studio (5,000), Max (12,500) and Max 2x (25,000).
- Plan cards show the monthly cost. Annual billing is the default, with one Annual / Monthly switch above the plans.
- If you're on one of the earlier plans, nothing changes: you keep your price and weekly credits, and your plan now reads "(legacy)". You can switch to any of the new plans from Change Plan.

### Buying credits

- Credits are still $3 per 1,000 and never expire. Purchases now start at 5,000 credits ($15).
- Before checkout, Greysound shows what the same credits would cost on each plan, so you can compare before you buy.

### Rewards

- Daily reward credits have ended, for free accounts and subscribers alike. The Early Adopter card is gone too.

### Fixes

- Subscribe and Manage subscription work again in Pro.
- When checkout can't start, Pro now tells you why instead of showing "no checkout URL returned".

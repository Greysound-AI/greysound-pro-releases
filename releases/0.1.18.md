## Greysound Pro 0.1.18

- Added Bars and Beats duration controls when creating AI samples. New samples default to four bars, and bar lengths follow the session time signature.
- Expanded CoProducer support for installed Audio Unit and VST3 plug-ins, including discovering controls, making parameter changes, and finding official product guidance.
- Improved reliability when saving, reopening, and switching large or plug-in-heavy sessions while preserving third-party plug-in settings.
- Added clearer progress updates while sessions load, including tracks, plug-ins, clips, and other session content.
- Improved CoProducer recovery when a response is interrupted and made error messages more useful.
- Improved the accuracy of credit activity shown in Greysound Pro.

# Greysound Pro 0.2.4

Clearer AI credit handling: requests you can't afford are stopped before they start, and CoProducer tells you why.

### AI credits

- AI audio, editing, description, voice, and stem requests start only when your balance covers the request's estimate. If it doesn't, the Insufficient Credits dialog opens and nothing is charged. Requests whose length is only known afterward need a positive balance to start.
- If a request's final cost comes in above its estimate, the full amount is charged, even if that takes your balance below zero. New requests then wait until you add or earn credits.
- When a request needs more credits than you have, CoProducer tells you how many it needs and how many you have, and offers a shorter or cheaper option instead of retrying.
- If a charge for a result you already received can't be completed right away, the credits indicator shows a dot and Settings > Credits & usage shows Charge pending while it is retried. Your balance updates when it goes through; if it can't be completed within an hour, you are not charged for that result.

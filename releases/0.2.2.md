# Greysound Pro 0.2.2

Improved section labels and navigation in generated audio previews.

- Section labels stay aligned with their waveform markers, including short sections.
- Overlapping labels move onto another line so they remain readable. The layout adapts when the preview width changes.
- Click a section label to jump to its start time.
- These improvements apply to previews in CoProducer and Cover Assist.

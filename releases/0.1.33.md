# Greysound Pro 0.1.33

A reliability update: crossfades and fades now survive every edit, undo and reload; aux sends, Master track plug-ins and stem bounces work as they should; Mute and Solo behave like Pro Tools; and CoProducer chats are now kept in your Greysound account, with a copy on your computer so they open instantly and stay readable offline.

### Crossfades & fades
- Fades now travel with the clip they're on, and a crossfade moves only when both of its clips move together. Moving a crossfaded clip away from its partner removes the crossfade, a move that shortens the join shrinks it, and moving a clip next to another no longer takes over that clip's own fade-out.
- Deleting or splitting a clip that is part of a crossfade no longer leaves its neighbour fading in from silence or fading out into nothing.
- Crossfades saved in a session are restored when you open it. Previously sessions with many clips could lose some crossfades on load, and the next save made the loss permanent. Crossfades from older sessions that no longer fit their clips are now adjusted to match Pro Tools (shortened to the available audio, or kept as a fade-in when the partner clip is gone) instead of being dropped.

### Editing & undo
- Undo now restores crossfades that an edit broke, whether the edit was a move, trim, split, delete or recording, and including trims made by CoProducer.
- Undo now brings back a clip's loop settings and waveform after a cut, delete, move, trim or split, for your own edits and CoProducer's alike.
- Each session now has its own undo history. Opening, switching to or creating a session starts with empty Undo and Redo, so Redo can no longer replay the previous session's edits on the new one.
- Clicking a clip and pressing Delete or Cmd+X now removes exactly that clip. It could leave a tiny sliver of the clip behind, or trim a sliver off its crossfaded neighbour, and copying a clip next to another could paste a zero-length piece of the neighbour.
- Undo and Redo after a cut are labelled "Cut" instead of "Delete".
- Pasting a clip copied from another session now copies its audio into the current session. Previously the pasted region had no audio and the session refused to save until the paste was undone.
- The track context menu now offers only what applies to the track you right-clicked. An audio clip selected on another track no longer adds Invert phase, Clip gain or Time stretch to an instrument track's menu.
- Pressing Escape while dragging out a selection cancels it without creating a selection or resuming playback.
- Escape now closes only the topmost open menu, including the Grid and Nudge value menus, which previously ignored it. It no longer also closes a dialog or panel underneath.

### MIDI editor
- Cmd+A in the piano roll selects only the notes of the open region and no longer also selects every region on the timeline, where the next Delete could remove the whole session. Cmd+Delete in the piano roll does nothing instead of deleting the timeline selection.
- Select All selects only the notes you can see, not notes hidden by trimming the region.
- Paste puts notes at the playhead and Duplicate puts the copy right after the selection, instead of one grid step after the originals. The region grows to fit notes that land past its end, instead of hiding them.
- Shift+Left/Right extends the timeline selection even while the piano roll is open, and the Logic scheme's Cmd+Left/Right zoom is no longer swallowed by the editor.

### Mixing & plug-ins
- Aux sends now carry audio. Previously creating a send in Greysound Pro failed and nothing reached the aux return.
- Plug-ins can be added to the Master track. Previously every Master insert failed with a "Couldn't Load" error.
- Mute and Solo are independent, as in Pro Tools. Turning one on no longer turns the other off, a track that is both muted and soloed stays silent, and the buttons now respond immediately and switch back if the change could not be applied.
- New sessions start with the Master fader at 0 dB instead of playing 3 dB quiet until the session was reopened, and a fader at unity reads "0" instead of "-0.0".
- When a plug-in fails to load, the error dialog suggests reinstalling or rescanning only when the plug-in itself is the problem. Other failures say what went wrong.

### Bouncing
- Bounce Tracks (Stems) renders each track under its own name. Previously the first five tracks failed with "Bounce Failed" and the rest were rendered from the wrong track, so stems could be silently mislabelled. This also fixes baked-in aux sends and bouncing a region with its channel strip.
- After Bounce Mix, saving in the Save panel is the end of it: the dialog says where the mix was saved, with Show in Finder, instead of asking you to save it again.
- The Bounce Mix Save panel opens in the session's bounced-files folder instead of the Desktop.
- Bounce Mix reports the length of the file it actually wrote, not the planned length including the tail allowance.

### Sessions & saving
- Choosing "Custom location" for a new session now asks for the folder right away and shows where the session will be created. Creating the session makes a new folder there; it no longer opens another session that happened to be in that folder. This works from the start screen, File > New Session and the session picker.
- Clicking inside the session picker's New Session dialog no longer closes the picker and throws the dialog away.
- "Open an existing session from disk" only accepts `.gss` files and opens exactly the file you picked.
- A clip whose audio file is missing stays in the session as an offline region, drawn dimmed with a dashed outline and no waveform, instead of disappearing. The missing file is reported however the session was opened, and saving keeps the region so it plays again once the file is back.
- If part of a session fails to load, saving it no longer deletes the parts that failed.
- Important notices, such as parts of a session that didn't load, a refused save, recording warnings or a failed import, now appear in a dialog that stays open until you dismiss it instead of a toast that vanished after three seconds.
- Switching to a session that has no regions no longer leaves the previous session's regions on the timeline.
- Renamed clips keep their names after a reload, and AI-generated clips keep their link to the generation that made them.
- Switching sessions while a save is still running no longer marks the new session as saved, shows it as saving, or shows the previous session's save error. A failed autosave is now retried automatically, so edits made during the failed save aren't left unsaved.
- The missing audio devices dialog names the saved device (for example your headphones) instead of "Unknown Device".
- The tracks area no longer slides sideways for a moment when a session opens.
- Sign-in forms, "Publish to Showcase", "Update Preview", Artist Name, Tags and the showcase Username field, which don't work in Greysound Pro, are no longer offered there. If you're signed out with a session open, the panel tells you to return to My Sessions to sign in.

### CoProducer
- Your chats are now stored in your Greysound account. Greysound Pro keeps a copy on your computer, so chats appear instantly when you open a session and can still be read offline.
- Chats you had only on this computer from earlier versions stay listed and are uploaded to your account the next time you continue them.
- Saving a chat while you're offline shows a "You're offline" dialog instead of failing silently. Chat errors no longer switch the app into its "Offline, using saved sign-in" state.
- When a long conversation outgrows CoProducer's memory, the full history is kept: a thin "Earlier context summarised" line marks where the summary begins, and a reply that was cut short is labelled "Interrupted, continued below".
- CoProducer can now see each region's fades and crossfades, so it no longer reports "no crossfade" on a crossfaded region.
- Loops that CoProducer sets on audio clips now show their loop markers and use the right length for the waveform, and CoProducer reports the loop it actually made. Loops set this way in older sessions display correctly too.
- "+" and History are disabled while CoProducer is working and until its reply is saved, so a reply can no longer end up in the wrong chat or be lost. A message sent right after "+" starts a genuinely empty chat instead of carrying over the previous conversation.
- Opening a session with no chats no longer creates an empty "New Chat"; a chat is created when you send your first message.
- A message you typed but didn't send no longer follows you into another session.
- A chat whose deletion was interrupted is hidden and finishes deleting automatically.

### Window & app
- The splash screen's fade-in plays smoothly instead of stuttering and jumping while the app starts.
- On macOS, the "Fill code from Messages" suggestion no longer pops up over Greysound's dialogs, such as Add Track.

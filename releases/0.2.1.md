# Greysound Pro 0.2.1

Meet Aria, a new audio model for songs with vocals and instrumentals, at 30% fewer audio-generation credits than Sonata.

### Audio models

- Choose Aria in CoProducer and supported generation, editing, Cover Assist, and stem-separation flows. Generate songs, remix vocal recordings, replace lyrics in a selected range, and add intros or outros.
- Aria chooses the natural duration of generated songs. For exact-duration generation, detailed section composition, or edits Aria does not support, choose Sonata II.
- Budget is now Prelude, Premium Classic is Sonata I, and Premium is Sonata II. Prelude now uses 50% fewer audio-generation credits than Sonata.
- Model controls now use the labels Audio Model and CoProducer Model. Credit history also shows the audio model names.

### Covers and previews

- Choose the audio model before starting Cover Assist. With Aria, uploaded audio serves as the reference for a new arrangement; preview the result before adding it to your session.
- Generated previews show timed section markers when available, with readable labels for short sections.
- Cover previews can be played again after returning to the panel, including when the original playback link has expired.

### Reliability

- Aria waits and retries when generation capacity is busy. Accepted requests resume without starting another paid generation.
- CoProducer keeps generation progress in chat. Stem separation and adding the completed stems to the session appear as separate steps.
- Retrying a failed stem import reuses the prepared stems. Definite stem-generation rejections can be retried after the problem is resolved.
- Improved saving after generated audio is added to a session, and fixed MP3 reference-duration detection for audio transformations.

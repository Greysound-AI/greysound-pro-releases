# Greysound Pro 0.1.29

A startup and sign-in update: the app now opens straight onto the session picker or sign-in screen with no in-between loading screen, keeps working offline with your saved sign-in, and tells you why when you do need to sign in again. Plus fixes for text-field clipboard menus, tooltips, CoProducer audio previews, and per-session workspace memory.

### Startup & sign-in
- The splash screen now covers the entire startup. Previously the main window appeared while the app was still initialising and showed an "Initializing Greysound Pro" line before the session picker arrived. The window now opens only once the picker or sign-in screen is fully ready, and the splash reports each startup step along the way.
- Greysound Pro works offline with your saved sign-in. Starting the app never waits on the network; if the sign-in server can't be reached, your saved account is used and the picker shows "Offline, using saved sign-in". Your sign-in is re-checked automatically the next time the app can reach the server.
- If your sign-in has been revoked (for example after changing your password on another device, or if the account was disabled or deleted), the app now signs you out and shows why on the sign-in screen, with your email prefilled. Previously a revoked sign-in kept "working" locally while every cloud feature quietly failed.
- If the sign-in is revoked while a session is open, a banner appears and cloud features pause; your session stays open and saves locally, and you're asked to sign in again when you return to the session picker.
- The built-in FX Designer no longer reports "Your sign-in has expired" after about an hour in a session where you hadn't used other AI features. Its window now refreshes the sign-in the same way the main window does.

### Sessions
- Each session now remembers its workspace: zoom level, waveform zoom, timeline scroll position, grid value and modifier, active tool, snap-to-grid, and follow-playhead. They come back exactly as you left them when you reopen the session, like Pro Tools. Changing them doesn't mark the session as unsaved.
- The session name in the title bar shows the unsaved dot and saving spinner in the same place and style as the web app.

### Editing & UI
- Right-clicking a text field (the CoProducer box, rename fields, FX Designer chat) now shows Cut, Copy, Paste, and Select All. Previously nothing appeared.
- Ctrl+V pastes into text fields again on Windows and Linux.
- Knob and fader tooltips now show just the control's name, sit bottom-right of the cursor, and hide while you drag, so they no longer cover the live value readout. Controls whose tooltip carried extra information (delay note time, sample player readouts) now show it directly.
- The interface scales by display size rather than window size, so narrowing the window no longer shrinks the whole UI.
- On Windows, the native minimize, maximize, and close buttons no longer paint over the in-app title bar buttons and the credit balance.

### CoProducer
- Clicking or dragging on an audio preview waveform now puts the playhead exactly under the cursor; it used to land up to about 11 px off near the edges.
- Seeking on a preview card that isn't currently playing now plays and seeks that card's own version instead of jumping the other card's playback.
- The Cover panel keeps its title, artist, lyrics edits, prompt, and reference file when you collapse and reopen the side panel.
- Chats under "Older" in the history menu now show their last-updated date.

## Greysound Pro 0.1.23

- The AI Co-Producer can now export your song: ask it to bounce or download the mix and it runs the Bounce flow for you, leaving you one click away from saving the file.
- The AI Co-Producer can now print the full mix onto a new track in your session ("print the mix"), giving you a visible, editable waveform of the final master inside the project.
- The AI Co-Producer no longer duplicates sends, effect buses, or tracks when asked to fix something that already exists. It now verifies the session state first and adjusts the existing routing instead of recreating it.
- When what you hear disagrees with what the AI believes about your session, it now investigates (solo and mute states, send levels, playhead position) and reports a suspected problem instead of rebuilding your routing.
- Fixed the stems bounce progress display briefly showing an incorrect track count while audio was being prepared.

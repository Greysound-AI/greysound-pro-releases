# Greysound Pro 0.2.6

A big mixing, editing, and sound-quality release: Solo Safe, duplicate track, Grid/Slip switching, typed fader levels, stereo instrument panning, live meters in the dynamics and EQ plug-ins, and built-in instruments and effects that sound the same in Greysound Pro and on the web.

### Mixing

- Solo Safe, as in Pro Tools: Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) a track's S button so the track stays audible while you solo others. A Solo Safe track shows a shield with an S on its S button, and the setting is saved with the session.
- Each mixer strip, Master included, shows its level in dB under the fader. Click the readout to type an exact level, such as -6 or +2.5. While you drag a fader, its value appears beside the handle.
- Option-click (Alt-click on Windows and Linux) a fader, its level readout, or the volume knob in a narrow mixer to return the track to 0 dB. This is now a single undo step, and it writes automation when Read automation controls the volume.
- Instrument tracks are now stereo, with two pan knobs like a stereo audio track. When you open an older session, its instrument tracks get the two knobs, and any pan automation on them is removed with a notice so you can redraw it.
- Every track with two pan knobs has Link and Inverse buttons, as in Pro Tools. With Link on, both knobs move together; with Inverse also on, they move in opposite directions.
- New Pan Depth setting in File > Session Settings (-2.5, -3.0, -4.5, or -6.0 dB, as in Pro Tools) sets how much a centred pan turns each side down. It applies to mono tracks, to each knob of a stereo pair, and to sends. It is saved with the session, and Export Selected Tracks keeps it.
- In Greysound Pro, inserts on an aux return now always come after the signal from its sends, so parallel compression works even when the send was created first. Sends from instrument tracks now carry both channels instead of dropping the right one.
- On the web, muting a track, or soloing another track, now also silences its pre-fader sends, as in Greysound Pro.
- On the web, plug-ins that add latency, such as the Guitar and Bass Amp, are now compensated, so their tracks and the metronome stay in time with the rest of the mix. A bypassed plug-in adds no delay.

### Editing

- The new Edit mode menu, first in the toolbar, switches between Slip and Grid. It replaces the small snap icon in the track headers.
- Hold Cmd (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) while you drag to use the other edit mode for that drag only. This works for regions, MIDI notes, selections, markers, and the playhead. A badge next to the pointer shows the temporary mode.
- Duplicate a track from the Track menu, from the track's context menu, with a shortcut (Option+Shift+D with Pro Tools shortcuts, Cmd+D/Ctrl+D with Logic or Ableton shortcuts), or by Option-dragging (Alt-dragging) its header. The copy keeps the regions, inserts, sends, automation, routing, and mixer settings, and it is a single undo step.
- The Track menu now works like Pro Tools' and acts on the selected tracks: New Track..., Duplicate, Delete, Rename..., and Show Automation. On macOS it is in the menu bar; on Windows and Linux, in the title bar; on the web, under the session name.
- MIDI note names follow Pro Tools numbering, where middle C (MIDI 60) is C3, in the piano roll, on the drum pads, and on the Virtual MIDI Keyboard. The piano roll's keyboard now looks like a piano, with short black keys, and each pitch keeps its own note lane.

### Instruments

- The Drum Machine and Drum Sampler have a Pads grid. Each pad is labelled with its General MIDI drum name, note name, and number, and plays the sound when you press it. When a track plays a drum instrument, the piano roll shows the drum names next to its keys.
- When the Virtual MIDI Keyboard plays a drum instrument, each key shows a drum symbol and a short name such as KICK, SNR, or OH. In Greysound Pro, third-party drum plug-ins that name their keys get the same labels on the keyboard and in the piano roll.
- New hi-hat and cymbal sounds in the Drum Machine, with a separate sound for every General MIDI hat, crash, ride, bell, splash, and china key. The pedal hat now chokes the open hat. Hat and cymbal Decay now sets the time until the sound dies away. Kits that still use the old default Decay values switch to the new defaults.
- The six General MIDI toms now play six rising pitches, from low floor tom to high tom. Tom Drive now works.
- Both drum instruments play exactly the drum map their pads show: keys outside the map are silent on both editions. A closed hat cuts off an open hat with a short 30 ms fade instead of a click, hits ring out fully before fading, and Drum Sampler volume and pan knobs now affect hits that are already ringing.
- Synth: the HPF and LPF Slope, Drift, Dust, and Amp and Filter Env Velocity knobs now work on both editions. Filter Env Velocity starts at 0%, so existing patches sound as before.
- Synth on the web: Ring Mod, Pulse Width, Env > Shape, Amp Shape, Drive, Resonance, the LFO, and the modulation matrix now work and sound as they do in Greysound Pro.
- Synth in Greysound Pro: the LFO runs at its set rate however many notes you hold, the LFO Shape buttons work, the filters no longer darken as you play more notes, and new Synths start at the 5 Hz LFO rate the knob shows.
- The arpeggiator follows the session tempo and starts on the beat on both editions. In Greysound Pro, Up-Down starts on the lowest note, each new chord starts the pattern again, and each step plays at the velocity of its key.
- Release now lasts as long as the knob says on the Synth and the Instrument Sampler. Turning Release while a note is fading changes the rest of that fade.
- Instrument Sampler in Greysound Pro: every note plays in tune, each of the four slots has its own envelope, and Tone works. On the web, velocity, Decay, Release, Tone, and chord levels now behave as they do in Greysound Pro.
- Built-in instruments no longer clip: each one has a fixed output level with a gentle safety limit, on both editions. When an instrument runs out of voices, the note it replaces fades out over 5 ms instead of clicking.
- On the web, MIDI notes are timed by the audio clock, so Drum Machine and sampler parts no longer sound choppy when the browser is busy, and playback sounds exactly like the bounce. In Greysound Pro, the Synth and drum instruments use less CPU.

### Effects

- Gain-reduction meters in the Compressor, Limiter, Noise Gate, Expander, and De-Esser hold their peak briefly. A live dot rides the curve, and a 3-second strip shows the input level, output level, and gain reduction.
- The EQ shows a live spectrum behind its curve, and the Stereo Imager has a live stereo scope. These displays work in the web app and in Greysound Pro's plug-in windows.
- The EQ's High-Pass and Low-Pass bands use a Slope knob (6, 12, 18, or 24 dB/oct) and can switch to Low Notch and High Notch, as in Pro Tools EQ III. Shelf Q runs from 0.1 to 2.0, and the EQ graph now draws exactly what you hear.
- On the web, the Compressor, Limiter, Noise Gate, Expander, and De-Esser now use the same processing as Greysound Pro and add no delay, so parallel compression no longer sounds phasey. The Limiter's Ceiling is now a true ceiling on both editions.
- De-Esser: Split mode now turns down the whole sibilant band, and the graph shows the band that is actually reduced. The controls use Pro Tools names: Range is the amount of reduction and Width is the band.
- Delay: new Mono mode, also listed as Mono Delay in the plug-in menu. Delay times reach 5 seconds at any sample rate. Changing the time crossfades instead of clicking. On the web, the left and right channels echo separately, and high Feedback can no longer build up without limit.
- Delay, Auto Filter, Tremolo, and Auto Pan share one list of 16 note values for tempo sync, including dotted, triplet, 1/32, 2/1, and 4/1. Auto Filter, Tremolo, and Auto Pan can now sync to tempo, locked to the bar. New Auto Pans start at Phase 180° so they sweep from side to side.
- Reverb: new Tail EQ low and high cuts on the reverb signal only. Decay now sets how long the tail lasts, and Size sets how far apart the reflections are, on both editions.
- Guitar Amp and Bass Amp now use every control on both editions, with the same amp model in Greysound Pro and on the web.
- Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Vibrato, Overdrive, Tape Saturation, Bitcrusher, Stereo Spreader, Utility, Auto Filter, and Tremolo now sound the same on both editions. Many of them also had their own fixes, such as Flanger sweeps that no longer stall and a Utility that no longer turns stereo audio down at centre pan.
- Turning a knob or automating a parameter while playing now glides smoothly instead of clicking. On the web, effect automation is now timed by the audio clock.
- In Greysound Pro, every built-in effect now uses the ranges and defaults its panel shows. Some older Pro sessions may sound slightly different because they now play what their menus always showed, for example the De-Esser's Split and Wideband modes, the Chorus voice count, and Auto Filter Resonance.

### FX Designer

- The delay, chorus, flanger, and phaser modules have a Sync to tempo switch with a note selector. Synced effects stay locked to the transport and glide smoothly when the tempo changes. The delay module's Time now goes up to 5000 ms.
- Modules sound the same on the web and in Greysound Pro: on the web the phaser now sweeps, the reverb is a real reverb, delay repeats land on time, and the saturator and transient shaper work as intended. The limiter module never goes over its ceiling, and a gate with Hold set to 0 ms opens.
- In Greysound Pro, changing a module or automating it no longer restarts the effect, so delay and reverb tails keep ringing.

### Recording

- If one armed track can't record, the other armed tracks still record, and a warning names the track that didn't. If recording can't start at all, a message explains why.
- Greysound Pro tells you when it can't get sound from your input instead of recording silence: when macOS blocks the microphone, when an input sends only silence, or when an input stops responding.
- Low-Latency Monitoring in Greysound Pro is now off unless you choose AUTO in Session Settings > Audio Driver, as in Pro Tools. With AUTO, arming a track or starting playback resets the audio device, which can cause a dropout.
- Greysound Pro sets your audio device to the session's sample rate when the session opens, as Pro Tools does. If another app changes the rate, playback stops and a dialog asks what to do. An older session saved without a sample rate keeps the device's current rate the first time it opens.
- On macOS, fewer audio dropouts when your input and output are different devices, and takes recorded through the MacBook's built-in microphone now land in time.
- Choosing an audio input or output in Session Settings or in Calibrate Latency now switches the device right away.

### Browser support

- Safari, iPhone, and iPad visitors see a notice recommending Chrome or Greysound Pro, starting on the sign-in screen.
- In Safari, record-armed tracks now record the on-screen virtual keyboard. Safari can't connect to MIDI keyboards, so Session Settings now says so instead of showing an empty list.
- In Safari, the Inserts and Sends tabs now scroll down to every slot. On any browser, a sideways trackpad swipe over a fader or knob now scrolls the mixer.
- Audio devices the browser doesn't name now show readable names, such as System default output.
- On the web, each audio clip is decoded only once, so sessions load faster and use less memory.

### Analysis and CoProducer

- Analyze audio is faster: it reads only the part of the clip the region plays.
- Tempo detection is more accurate on both editions: held chords no longer throw it off, fast and slow songs are read at the tempo musicians would write, and audio without a beat gets no tempo.
- CoProducer can measure the frequency content of your tracks and compare it before and after its changes. An Analyzing frequencies card shows its progress.
- CoProducer can pan and automate both pan knobs of a stereo or instrument track, including Link and Inverse.

### Fixes

- Export Selected Tracks now works in sessions that contain audio. The new session gets its own copy of the exported clips' audio (and, in Greysound Pro, of third-party plug-in states), and the session you were working in stays open.
- Bounced stems of tracks routed into an aux or bus are no longer silent. Bounces now include the full tail of FX Designer chains, and in Greysound Pro also the repeats of the built-in Delay.
- A failed session rename or duplicate shows "Couldn't rename session" or "Couldn't duplicate session". In Greysound Pro, a session whose folder can't be read no longer opens as an empty session that could be saved over the real one.
- Greysound Pro's Library sync no longer loses unsynced edits when an item can't be read, and one failing item no longer stops the rest from syncing.
- If Keep cloud or Keep both fails in Greysound Pro's sync conflict dialog, you are returned to the session list with "Couldn't open session". Keep both no longer copies a session without files it couldn't read.
- In Greysound Pro, plug-in windows float above the main window, as in Pro Tools, so they no longer disappear behind it. The plug-in menu opens upward when the insert slot is near the bottom of the window instead of being cut off.
- On the web, switching sessions from inside the DAW no longer leaves the previous session loaded in memory, and pressing Play again no longer sometimes resets insert settings.

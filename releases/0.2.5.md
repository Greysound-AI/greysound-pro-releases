# Greysound Pro 0.2.5

Hear CoProducer's Sonata II and Aria generations while they are still being made, played natively by Greysound Pro.

### Live generations

- Sonata II and Aria generations stream into a live card in the chat as soon as audio is ready, so you can listen before the generation is saved. Press play on the card to listen; to start listening automatically, choose Auto-play in Settings > AI CoProducer > New Generations.
- Streamed audio is fetched and played by Greysound Pro itself on macOS, Windows, and Linux. Playback continues through the pauses Aria takes while it composes.
- The live card shows its composing animation from the moment a generation starts, and both models report progress the same way: Composing, then Streaming.
- If you press play before audio is ready, or playback catches up with the audio received so far, the play button shows a spinner instead of looking unresponsive.
- When the generation is saved, the live card stays in place until the saved card's waveform is ready, then swaps without a flicker. Sonata II's saved card reuses the waveform that streamed in, so it appears at once.

### Chat

- Pressing Stop while CoProducer waits for an Aria generation ends the wait. A generation that has already started still finishes and is charged, and CoProducer is told so, so it doesn't start a replacement unless you ask.
- A generation cut off by a reload or a closed window, or stopped, is labelled Interrupted or Stopped instead of failed, with "output recovered below" when its result appears as Recovered audio. A generation that is still running no longer also appears as Recovered audio.
- When CoProducer calls a tool incorrectly, the tool row shows why instead of "Interrupted".

### Fixes

- Saving several AI generations close together no longer risks one save overwriting another or dropping a generation from the session's list.

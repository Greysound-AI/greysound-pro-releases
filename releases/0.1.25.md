## Greysound Pro 0.1.25

- Fixed soloing a track silencing its own reverb and delay. Aux and bus return tracks now stay audible while a solo is active, so soloing a vocal lets you hear it with its effects — matching how solo works in the web version and in other professional DAWs.
- Bounced stems are now saved under their track names (for example `Lead Vox.wav` instead of a generated file name), and re-bouncing never overwrites an earlier take — repeated bounces get ` (1)`, ` (2)` added to the name.
- Fixed the stems bounce completion dialog reporting "0 tracks" with a download button that did nothing. On Pro, stems save directly into your session's `bounced-files` folder; the dialog now shows how many stems were saved, where they are, and offers a one-click "Show in Finder" (or "Show in File Explorer" on Windows) to open the folder.

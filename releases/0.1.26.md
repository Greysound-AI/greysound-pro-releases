## Greysound Pro 0.1.26

A large stability and workflow update focused on long-standing bug reports.

### Audio & sessions
- Fixed audio not coming back after your Mac wakes from sleep. Previously the playhead would move but no sound would play until you restarted the app; playback now recovers automatically after wake.
- Fixed a serious bug where a session could lose all of its regions after a plugin crash or a failed clip load. If anything in a session can't be restored, the rest of the session now loads normally and a notice tells you exactly what couldn't be loaded — and Greysound will refuse to save a session in a state that would wipe your work.
- Fixed third-party plugins that crashed on load becoming impossible to remove — they no longer reappear after deleting them and saving.
- Plugins are now matched strictly by their identity when loading a session, so a session can no longer silently load the wrong plugin that happens to share a name.

### Editing & transport
- Moving the playhead no longer stops playback. Clicking a new spot on the timeline or in a region while playing continues playing from there; if playback is stopped, it stays stopped. Dragging the playhead or making a selection pauses playback while you drag and resumes when you let go — the same behavior as other professional DAWs.
- Cut, Copy, Paste, and Delete now work on selected regions from the Edit menu and with the standard keyboard shortcuts (Cmd+X/C/V). Greysound Pro also now has a proper macOS menu bar.
- Fixed typing sometimes not registering until you clicked around — most noticeable after resizing the window or leaving fullscreen.
- Changing your keyboard shortcut scheme (Ableton, Pro Tools, Logic) in Settings now takes effect immediately, without restarting the app.
- Zooming with the scroll wheel or trackpad now stays centered on your cursor, and slow trackpad pinches zoom smoothly instead of doing nothing. Scrolling while hovering over the timeline ruler now zooms; scrolling over the tracks still scrolls.
- Dragging the playhead, dragging a selection, or seeking with the arrow keys past the edge of the window now scrolls the timeline automatically — no need to turn on Follow Playhead.
- Arrow keys now move the playhead by your current grid setting (and work correctly in any time signature) instead of always jumping a fixed amount. Hold Option to jump a full bar, and hold Shift to extend a time selection with the arrow keys.
- Pressing Escape while dragging, trimming, fading, or looping a region now cancels the edit and puts everything back.
- Clicking inside a region now places the playhead exactly where you clicked instead of jumping to the start of the region.
- Fixed white outline artifacts appearing around the record button and other controls after clicking them.
- The record-arm button now reflects your click immediately and recovers correctly if arming fails.

### CoProducer & previews
- Fixed chat history disappearing. Chats can no longer be accidentally overwritten when history fails to load — if that happens you'll now see a clear "Couldn't load chat history" notice with a Retry button instead of an empty chat. Messages sent right before quitting the app are no longer lost, and on Pro your chats are now stored inside the session's project folder.
- Fixed cover song previews playing on top of each other. Starting any preview now stops the previous one, everywhere in the app, and takes are listed in the order they were generated.

### Credits
- Fixed the daily credit reward not being granted when the app was already open when the day rolled over. If you keep Greysound open across midnight, your next edit, playback, or CoProducer message that day claims the reward — simply leaving the app open idle does not.

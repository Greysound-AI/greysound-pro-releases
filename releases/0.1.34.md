# Greysound Pro 0.1.34

Get help without leaving your session, find your preferences in the redesigned Settings window, and enjoy smoother CoProducer conversations.

**Updating on macOS:** If you're using 0.1.33, download and install 0.1.34 manually. This release restores automatic updates for future versions.

### Greysound Manual

- Browse and search the manual in Settings > Greysound Manual, including screenshots, diagrams, keyboard shortcuts, and troubleshooting guides. The manual is available offline.
- Open relevant manual sections from Learn more links throughout the app, or ask CoProducer for instructions tailored to your session.
- Manual source links appear beneath each CoProducer answer once it finishes. Links from older conversations automatically open the current equivalent article and section when available.
- Use chapters, section links, Back and Forward, and Previous and Next article controls to find your way around. Opening the chapter list brings it into view immediately.
- Keyboard shortcuts used while reading or searching the manual no longer trigger edits in your session.

### Settings and account

- A dedicated Settings window brings your profile, credits, preferences, plug-ins, keyboard shortcuts, and manual together, with search to help you find what you need.
- Access your account and Settings from the Pro title bar, including before you open a session.
- Tooltips appear beneath their controls again; knob and fader tooltips continue to follow the pointer.

### CoProducer

- Product-help answers get straight to the instructions, with references available through the manual source links. Proactive offers describe what CoProducer can do in ordinary language.
- Improved reliability when continuing long or interrupted conversations. Continue resumes the unfinished response, and sending messages quickly no longer creates duplicate messages.
- Adding generated audio is more reliable when a download is interrupted. Add clip to new track also works when the original source region is no longer in the session.

### Sessions and reliability

- Double-clicking a session file opens that session directly, even when Greysound Pro isn't already running.
- A temporary problem reading My Sessions no longer replaces your saved session list with an incomplete one.
- The plug-in menu opens more quickly, especially with large plug-in collections.
- If the interface unexpectedly goes blank, Greysound Pro stops playback and recording and attempts to reopen it. Repeated failures offer Reload or Quit, and quitting no longer gets stuck waiting for an unresponsive interface.

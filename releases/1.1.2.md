# Greysound Pro 1.1.2

Generated songs are corrected to the session key, plug-in knobs are easier to set precisely, and CoProducer no longer stops without an answer after thinking for a long time.

### Generated songs

- A generated song that comes out one or two semitones away from the session key is moved into key, in the same step that corrects its tempo. The original is kept as the first version.
- A song that is further off, or major where minor was asked for, is left as it is, and its card says what key it sounds like.

### CoProducer

- A reply that used all of its room thinking is answered once more in the same reply, and you are not charged for the attempt that only thought.
- CoProducer thinks for less time before answering, and longer answers have room to finish.
- Note names match the MIDI editor: middle C is C3.
- When CoProducer describes what it changed or where something happens in the session, it works that out from the actual positions and values.
- A refusal on copyright or terms grounds is explained instead of ending as a generic failure, and you are told when an edit's styles were changed for copyright.
- An edit whose plan would only keep the original audio is refused before any paid work.

### Chat

- The Ask or Agent mode and the Sampling or Programming workflow you last chose are remembered across restarts, new chats and new sessions.

### Plug-in windows

- Time and rate knobs give the low end of their range most of the travel, so short attack and release times are easy to set.
- Hold Command (Control on Windows and Linux) while dragging a knob for fine adjustment.
- Double-click a knob or its value to type one.
- Click a knob and use the arrow keys to step it.

### Tracks

- Rename Track opens with the whole name selected, so typing replaces it.

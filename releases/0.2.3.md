# Greysound Pro 0.2.3

Simpler CoProducer controls, updated AI credit pricing, and improved native measurement and export workflows.

### CoProducer and AI credits

- CoProducer now uses one conversation model. The Premium/Budget chat choice has been removed; new requests use standard CoProducer pricing (approximately equivalent to previous budget pricing), including requests with an older saved Budget preference. Audio generation model choices remain independent.
- Failed and cancelled operations are free. A successful summary or audio operation can still be charged once if the surrounding chat fails. Historical credit transactions retain their original charges.
- Added experimental documentation research and musical listening for short track or Master excerpts.

### Measurement and exports

- CoProducer can measure the Master mix's integrated LUFS and true peak, separately from RMS and sample peak, and verify playback over a requested beat range.
- Native loudness measurements run on a separate session copy, with progress shown in chat.
- CoProducer waits for the native export Save step to finish before reporting success and can analyze the saved bounce. Waiting for Save no longer requires another render.

### Chat and controls

- Research citations appear as compact source badges and open in your external browser.
- The chat settings button always shows a brain and the selected audio model name. Its styling matches the Agent and Sampling controls.
- Compact credit badges show amounts such as 21.8k; Credits & usage retains the full whole-credit amount.
- Improved handling of stopped chats and late tool results. Marker renames preserve their positions, and rejected effect-parameter batches report that no settings were applied.

### Audio reliability

- MIDI device changes no longer interrupt playback or an ongoing recording.
- Improved real-time performance of built-in instruments and effects.

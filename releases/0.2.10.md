# Greysound Pro 0.2.10

Invite friends to Greysound and earn credits when they join, and promote Greysound through the new Greysound Affiliate Program.

### New in Greysound Pro

- **Refer friends.** Settings › Refer friends, also in the account menu, has your personal invite link. Friends who have never paid for Greysound get 50% off their first payment, a subscription or a credit purchase. When their first payment goes through, you get credits worth half of what they paid. The page lists who joined with your link, who has paid, and the credits you earned.
- **Your referral discount.** If you joined through a friend's invite or an affiliate's link, Greysound tells you about your discount when you sign up, and Settings shows it until you use it, with the discounted price next to the full price on credit packs and subscriptions.
- **Affiliates.** Settings › Affiliates, also in the account menu, introduces the Greysound Affiliate Program: affiliates earn 50% of a new customer's first payment, and the people they bring get 50% off. Open the affiliate portal to join, get your link and set up payouts.

### Changes

- The Share from Showcase reward has ended. Sharing a link no longer earns 250 credits when someone signs up; invite friends from Settings › Refer friends instead.
